Selected Projects

A showcase of enterprise platforms, AI-powered tools, and FinTech experiences designed to scale.

Enterprise Financial Platform

Neo CFO

Complete UI/UX design for a robust enterprise financial platform tailored for complex reporting, forecasting, and analysis workflows.

Problem

Fragmented financial data visualization and convoluted legacy workflows hindering rapid decision-making for CFOs.

Approach

Restructured information architecture, designing an intuitive dashboard focused on enterprise usability and dense data clarity.

Outcome & Impact

Delivered a streamlined, scalable platform that significantly reduced workflow friction and set a new standard for internal data tools.

AI-Powered Platform

Spark AI

A modern interface design focused on enhancing user efficiency through AI-assisted workflows and seamless prompt experiences.

Problem

Users struggled to effectively interact with underlying AI models due to steep learning curves and overwhelming interfaces.

Approach

Designed guided prompt experiences and contextual AI-assisted workflows to lower the barrier to entry.

Outcome & Impact

Increased user efficiency and adoption rates by providing a modern, conversational, and highly intuitive interface.

Enterprise AI Workflow

Maiven

An AI-powered software platform demanding deep product thinking to balance complex enterprise requirements with user-first experiences.

Problem

Scaling AI capabilities within an enterprise environment without degrading the overall user experience.

Approach

Developed a scalable UI architecture emphasizing modularity, user-first interactions, and robust product strategy.

Outcome & Impact

Created a highly adaptable platform that seamlessly integrates complex AI tools into daily enterprise routines.

FinTech Product

Slice Credit Card

A mobile-first financial experience designed around the core pillars of trust, security, and absolute simplicity.

Problem

Traditional credit card management apps are often cluttered, inducing anxiety and reducing user trust.

Approach

Applied strict minimalist principles to mobile UX, prioritizing transparent data display and frictionless financial actions.

Outcome & Impact

Delivered a highly secure, simple-to-use application that bolstered user confidence and engagement.

In addition to the above, I have designed several other enterprise products across AI, FinTech, Productivity Platforms, and Internal Business Applications.
