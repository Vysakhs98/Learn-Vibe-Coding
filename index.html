Featured Projects

Neo CFO

Enterprise Financial Platform

A complete UI/UX overhaul focusing on complex financial data visualization and enterprise usability.

Problem

Fragmented financial data and convoluted reporting structures hindered efficient decision-making for executive teams.

Approach

Restructured the information architecture and designed modular dashboards to simplify complex workflows.

Outcome

A cohesive, intuitive enterprise platform that centralizes forecasting, reporting, and analysis.

Impact

Significantly reduced time-on-task for financial workflows and improved data comprehension across the organization.

Spark AI

AI-Powered Platform

Modern interface design centered around intuitive prompt experiences and AI-assisted workflows.

Problem

Users struggled to interact effectively with underlying AI models due to steep learning curves in prompt engineering.

Approach

Designed conversational, guided prompt experiences to abstract technical complexity and focus on user intent.

Outcome

A seamless, modern interface that makes AI assistance feel natural and integrated.

Impact

Dramatically increased user efficiency and adoption rates among non-technical user segments.

Maiven

Enterprise Software Platform

Scalable UI design applying robust product thinking to streamline enterprise AI operations.

Problem

Approach

Outcome

A robust, scalable UI architecture that elegantly houses complex enterprise workflows.

Impact

Future-proofed the platform, accelerating the design-to-development pipeline for subsequent feature releases.
